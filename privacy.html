Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Friday is a personal assistant used only by its owner on the owner's own computer.

What Friday accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): message senders, subjects, previews and, when asked, the text of a single message.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): event titles, times and locations.

How the data is used

The data is used only to answer the owner's own questions. To produce a spoken answer, the relevant text is sent to the AI model provider that powers the assistant. Friday does not sell, share or publish Google user data, does not use it for advertising, and does not store copies of emails or events beyond what is needed to answer a request.

Friday's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
